Tips that trip people up

  • Radius vs. ulna: the radius is on the thumb side. ("Radius rides with the thumb.") The ulna is on the pinky side and forms the point of your elbow.
  • Tibia vs. fibula: the tibia is the big shin bone on the inside (it carries your weight). The fibula is the thin one on the outside. ("The fibula is a fib: it's the little one.")
  • Carpals / metacarpals / phalanges (wrist, palm, fingers) mirror tarsals / metatarsals / phalanges (ankle, foot, toes). "Meta" = the middle one. Phalanges is the answer for both fingers and toes.
  • Sacrum vs. coccyx: the sacrum is the big triangle between the hip bones. The coccyx is the tiny tailbone below it.
  • Spelling counts on a practical: it's humerus (not "humorous"), phalanges, coccyx, clavicle.
